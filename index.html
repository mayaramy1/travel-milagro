About Us

Egypt's Premier Incoming Partner

Travel Milagro is a Travel Agency Class A devoted to Incoming Tourism in Egypt with license Number 1702, specialist in serving tour operators and travel agencies worldwide.

Our head office is located in Cairo, with dedicated offices and personal staff in Luxor, Aswan, Sharm El Sheikh and Hurghada. Our team consists of young and enthusiastic professionals with over 15 years of intensive experience.
